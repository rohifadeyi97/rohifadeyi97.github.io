03 / Creative approach

Made for the brief.
Finished for the platform.

The brief determines the edit. Restaurant work can afford close-ups and room to breathe; community promotions need clear event information and a sense of the crowd; vertical content needs a strong opening and an early payoff. I bring the same standard of photography, sound, color, and finishing to each format.
