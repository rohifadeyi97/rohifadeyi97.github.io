03 / About

Business systems.

I work across marketing, business systems, analytics, and creative production. At Chesapeake Region Safety Council, I develop content and campaigns for professional education and consulting while improving the workflows and reporting behind the work. My consulting and agency experience also includes campaigns across ecommerce, entertainment, and events.

Outside my day-to-day work, I build tools around problems I’ve encountered as a marketer and producer. Music Ops connects research, editorial planning, and production; Tandem explores AI-assisted editing with creative decisions in human hands.

I originally studied biology and biotechnology. That background still shapes how I research problems, test assumptions, and think about processes. I enjoy figuring out what could work better and building toward it.
