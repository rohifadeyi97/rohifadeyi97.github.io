02 / THE NEXT PROBLEM

From a lead to a client relationship.

SALESFORCE SANDBOX BUILD

The consulting branch exposed a gap. Routing a request solved the first response, but it didn't give staff a reliable place to track ongoing consulting work.

I built the next layer in Salesforce sandbox: a consulting Lead can convert into an Account, Contact, and custom Consultation Engagement record, with a follow-up Task. Accounts hold the client status; engagements preserve the history of work; a junction relationship supports multiple points of contact at the same company.

Scope: the core object model, relationships, flows, pages, list views, and permissions were implemented in sandbox. Renewal reminders were a planned follow-up, not part of the production claim.
