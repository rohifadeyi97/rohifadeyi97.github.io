Vinyl Club Forecast Model

A go-to-market and launch forecast for a proposed vinyl subscription brand extension — built to pressure-test the concept against real revenue and customer data before committing resources.

← Back to all projects

01

Overview

A brand extension proposal, not a guess.

Vinyl Club was proposed as a subscription-based brand extension — a tiered membership model built around curated vinyl, positioned to extend an existing customer base into a recurring-revenue product. Before committing sourcing budget or launch resources, the concept needed a forecast grounded in actual historical revenue and customer behavior rather than assumption.
