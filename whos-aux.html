Who's AUX Revenue Analytics

Revenue diagnostics across Shopify, Amazon, and TikTok advertising — tracing a period of declining performance back to the channel and product shifts driving it.

← Back to all projects

01

Overview

Where the revenue was slipping, and why.

Who's AUX sells across Shopify, Amazon, and TikTok Shop. Revenue had declined from a 2024 peak, but with three disconnected sales channels and no unified reporting layer, it wasn't clear which channel — or which products within it — was actually driving the drop. This project built the model needed to answer that question directly instead of guessing from three separate exports.

02

Approach

One model, three channels, no double-counting.

The core challenge was structural, not visual: Shopify, Amazon, and TikTok each define orders, refunds, and product identifiers differently. A star schema normalized all three into shared fact and dimension tables, so a single set of DAX measures could answer questions like "which channel" and "which product" without channel-specific logic scattered across the report.

Data sources

Model
